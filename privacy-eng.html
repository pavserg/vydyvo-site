Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Thank you for using Vydyvo.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Vydyvo collects and how it is used.

Information We Collect

Vydyvo does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number to use the app.

Bookmarks, ratings, reviews, and other app data are stored locally on your device.

Analytics and Crash Reporting

Vydyvo uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used and improve its features.
  • Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports and diagnostic information that help identify and fix application issues.

These services may collect technical information such as:

  • Device model
  • Operating system version
  • App version
  • Language and region
  • Anonymous usage statistics
  • Crash logs and diagnostic information

This information is used solely to improve the stability, performance, and user experience of the app.

Movie Data

Vydyvo retrieves movie, TV show, and person information from TMDB (The Movie Database) when you browse or search within the app.

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

Data Sharing

Vydyvo does not sell your personal information.

Information collected through Firebase services is processed in accordance with Google's privacy practices.

Your Choices

You may stop all information collection by uninstalling the application.

You can also manage analytics and privacy settings through your device settings where applicable.
